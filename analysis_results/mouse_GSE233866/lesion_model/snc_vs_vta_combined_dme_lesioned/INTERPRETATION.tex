\subsection*{What question this answers, and why it's the sharpest one}

This is the most disease-\emph{and}-region-specific comparison in the
project: not baseline regional structure (\texttt{../../healthy\_baseline/snc\_vs\_vta\_preservation/}),
and not disease-vs-healthy within one region
(\texttt{../lesioned\_vs\_intact\_combined\_dme\_snc/}), but which genes and
modules are differentially active in SNc versus VTA specifically while
neurodegeneration is in progress --- the signal most directly relevant to a
region-selective treatment target, since a drug can only be
region-selective if there's something molecularly distinct to target in the
first place. SNc-lesioned (2{,}291 cells) and VTA-lesioned (1{,}720 cells)
are pooled into one 4{,}011-cell network before testing, following the same
pooled-network-then-\texttt{FindDMEs} pattern as the other formal DME test
in this project.

\subsection*{Result}

CACNA1D's module here is \textbf{blue} (1{,}021 genes), with
$\mathrm{kME}=0.32$, and it shows one of the strongest signals in the
entire table: $\mathrm{avg\_log2FC} = +13.34$, $p_{\mathrm{adj}} =
4.5\times10^{-123}$, higher in SNc. Eleven of twelve tested modules differ
significantly between the two regions during lesioning, which is expected
on its own --- SNc and VTA are fundamentally different neuron populations,
not disease-state variants of the same one, so almost any module would be
expected to differ somewhat --- but CACNA1D's own module being among the
most extreme values in that set (behind only tan, $+4.70$ at
$p_{\mathrm{adj}}=1.1\times10^{-271}$, and ahead of every other module by
$p_{\mathrm{adj}}$) is notable specifically because it is CACNA1D's module,
not an arbitrary one drawn from the same table.

\subsection*{Synthesis across the three lesion-arm results}

Taken together with the lesioned-vs-intact result in
\texttt{../lesioned\_vs\_intact\_combined\_dme\_snc/} and the standalone
kME trajectory across \texttt{../snc\_intact\_network/} (0.346) $\to$
\texttt{../snc\_lesioned\_network/} (0.398), this is the most direct
evidence this project has produced for something CACNA1D-network-specific
happening preferentially in the vulnerable region \emph{during} the
neurodegenerative process itself, rather than as a static baseline
difference: CACNA1D's own module is (a) more central to itself within SNc
than intact tissue, (b) significantly perturbed at the eigengene level by
lesioning within SNc, and (c) significantly more active in SNc than VTA
specifically once lesioning is underway. All three lines of evidence point
the same direction independently, which is stronger than any one of them
alone --- though all three still come from the same six animals and the
same 6-OHDA model, so they are not three independent replications of the
finding, just three different statistical angles on one dataset.

\subsection*{Caveats}

As with the other DME table, treat log2FC magnitude as directional evidence
only; module eigengenes can be negative or near-zero, which destabilizes
fold-change arithmetic even when the underlying $p$-value is sound --- this
applies to several of the more extreme log2FC values in this table (e.g.\
yellow at $+12.78$), not just the one explicitly flagged unstable (brown,
$p_{\mathrm{adj}}=0.068$, not even significant here). This network's blue
module (1{,}021 genes) is smaller than the lesioned-vs-intact network's
blue module (2{,}249 genes) despite pooling fewer total cells from a
different pairing of conditions, a reminder that module boundaries are
specific to each pooled network's own clustering run and are not
transferable between the two DME tests even though both happen to be
called ``blue.''
