\subsection*{Network construction}

VTA loses proportionally less of its population to lesioning than SNc does
(4{,}426 $\to$ 1{,}720 cells, 2.6$\times$, versus SNc's 3.8$\times$) ---
consistent with this dataset's premise that SNc is the more vulnerable
region. The resulting 1{,}720-cell network selects soft power 6
($R^2=0.939$), matching the intact-SNc network's power despite the different
cell population.

\subsection*{CACNA1D's position}

CACNA1D lands in the \textbf{blue} module (695 genes) with
$\mathrm{kME}=0.283$ --- weaker than either SNc lesion-arm value (intact:
0.346, lesioned: 0.398), placing lesioned-VTA closer to the healthy-baseline
VTA connectivity (0.296) than to anything in SNc. Blue's top hub genes reach
kME 0.53--0.71: \texttt{Hs6st3} (0.706), \texttt{Sorcs1} (0.640),
\texttt{Negr1} (0.579), \texttt{Tshz2} (0.574), \texttt{Zfp804a} (0.564),
\texttt{Tnr} (0.562), \texttt{Vip} (0.548), \texttt{Frmd5} (0.534) --- none
of which overlap with either SNc lesion-arm module's hub genes, or with
healthy-baseline VTA brown's hub genes. Only 18 of this module's 695 genes
(2.6\%) are shared with healthy-baseline VTA brown's 316 genes --- a level
of overlap similar to what two unrelated modules would show by chance,
reinforcing the pattern already seen at baseline (see
\texttt{../../healthy\_baseline/vta\_network/INTERPRETATION.tex}) that
VTA's CACNA1D-containing module is not a stable, reproducible community the
way SNc's is across every network in this project. Treat this network's
specific gene membership as a much less reliable estimate than the
equivalent SNc networks.

\subsection*{Enrichment}

Blue is enriched for \emph{Axon guidance} ($p_{\mathrm{adj}}=5.01\times10^{-4}$,
\texttt{KEGG\_2019\_Mouse}), \emph{Potassium ion transport} and
\emph{Potassium ion transmembrane transport} (both
$p_{\mathrm{adj}}=1.87\times10^{-3}$, GO), \emph{Regulation of cell
adhesion} ($p_{\mathrm{adj}}=1.87\times10^{-3}$, GO), \emph{Cell-cell
adhesion via plasma-membrane adhesion molecules}
($p_{\mathrm{adj}}=4.44\times10^{-3}$, GO), and \emph{Heparan sulfate
proteoglycan metabolic process} ($p_{\mathrm{adj}}=4.72\times10^{-3}$, GO).
The potassium-transport terms are new relative to any SNc module in this
project --- CACNA1D itself is a voltage-gated calcium channel, not
potassium, so this enrichment reflects the module's other members rather
than CACNA1D's own annotated function, and is one more data point that
VTA's lesioned network is organized around a genuinely different gene
community than SNc's.

\subsection*{Role in the region comparison}

This network is the standalone counterpart to the formal region-comparison
test in \texttt{../snc\_vs\_vta\_combined\_dme\_lesioned/}, which is where
the statistically-supported SNc-vs-VTA claim during lesioning is actually
established (CACNA1D's pooled-network module there is significantly more
active in SNc, $p_{\mathrm{adj}}=4.5\times10^{-123}$). This network's low
kME and near-zero gene-set overlap with its own healthy-baseline VTA
counterpart is consistent with, though not proof of, that formal result:
VTA's version of this program looks comparatively unstable and diffuse at
every stage this project measures it, alongside SNc's comparatively stable,
reproducible one.
