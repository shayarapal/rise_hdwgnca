The intact-hemisphere SNc network, built from 8{,}637 cells (the largest
single-condition population in this project), independently selects a soft
power of 6 ($R^2 = 0.959$) --- higher than the healthy-baseline SNc network's
power of 5, consistent with this being a different animal cohort under a
different experimental arm rather than a repeat of the same data.

CACNA1D falls in the \textbf{blue} module here with $\mathrm{kME}=0.346$, a
substantially stronger connectivity value than either healthy-baseline
region produced (SNc turquoise: 0.048; VTA brown: 0.094). This network exists
primarily as a qualitative counterpart to the formal statistical comparison
in \texttt{lesioned\_vs\_intact\_combined\_dme\_snc/}; its main use is as a
sanity check that CACNA1D's connectivity in intact tissue is not itself an
outlier before interpreting how it changes after lesioning.
