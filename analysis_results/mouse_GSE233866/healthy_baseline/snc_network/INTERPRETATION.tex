\subsection*{Network construction}

The soft-thresholding power scan selects $\beta=5$ as the lowest power clearing
the scale-free fit threshold ($R^2 \geq 0.8$; $R^2=0.801$ at $\beta=5$, versus
$0.684$ at $\beta=4$), giving a well-behaved, non-arbitrary basis for the
network rather than an assumed or copied value. Clustering on 2{,}226 pooled
SNc cells (6 untreated animals) yields 9 modules --- a modest number
consistent with a relatively homogeneous cell population, and much smaller
than VTA's 39 (see \texttt{../vta\_network/INTERPRETATION.tex} for why that
comparison is likely a sample-size artifact rather than a true difference in
regional organization).

\subsection*{CACNA1D's position}

CACNA1D is assigned to the \textbf{turquoise} module (1{,}352 genes) with
$\mathrm{kME}=0.442$ --- a moderate, non-trivial connection, though still
short of turquoise's top hub genes, which top out around kME
$0.83$--$0.87$: \texttt{Klhl1} (0.870), \texttt{Pbx1} (0.855),
\texttt{Frmd4b} (0.854), \texttt{Adarb2} (0.842), \texttt{Rgs6} (0.840),
\texttt{Slc35f1} (0.834), \texttt{Csmd1} (0.833), \texttt{Nos1ap} (0.829).
\emph{(Correction, 2026-08-13: this document previously reported
$\mathrm{kME}=0.048$, misread from the wrong \texttt{kME\_*} column
in \texttt{modules.csv}; 0.442 is \texttt{kME\_turquoise} for Cacna1d,
re-verified directly against the source file.)} CACNA1D is meaningfully
embedded in SNc's co-expression structure, not merely a peripheral member,
though it is a follower of this program rather than one of the genes
structurally defining it.

\subsection*{Enrichment}

This is reinforced by the enrichment result: turquoise is significantly
enriched for six terms across both libraries (all $p_{\mathrm{adj}} < 0.02$),
led by a tie between \emph{Dopaminergic synapse} and \emph{Axon guidance}
($p_{\mathrm{adj}} = 2.77\times10^{-4}$ each, \texttt{KEGG\_2019\_Mouse} ---
also corrected 2026-08-13 from the wrong-species \texttt{KEGG\_2021\_Human}
library), followed by \emph{Retrograde endocannabinoid signaling} and
\emph{Circadian entrainment} ($p_{\mathrm{adj}}=3.73\times10^{-3}$ each),
\emph{Amphetamine addiction} ($p_{\mathrm{adj}}=8.02\times10^{-3}$), and
\emph{Gap junction} ($p_{\mathrm{adj}}=1.63\times10^{-2}$). Both the module's
function and CACNA1D's moderate centrality within it point the same
direction: this is a real, biologically coherent synaptic/axon-guidance
program that CACNA1D is substantively part of, not a coincidental grouping
of unrelated genes.

\subsection*{Reproducibility across independently-built SNc networks}

Turquoise's gene membership is not a one-off artifact of this particular
clustering run. Two other SNc networks in this project --- built from an
entirely different cohort of 6 animals in the 6-OHDA lesion arm --- recover
substantially the same neighborhood: 1{,}009 of turquoise's 1{,}352 genes
(75\%) reappear in \texttt{snc\_intact\_network/}'s blue module, and 638
(47\%) reappear in \texttt{snc\_lesioned\_network/}'s brown module. All
three networks share a common core of 612 genes, including four of
turquoise's own top eight hub genes (\texttt{Klhl1}, \texttt{Pbx1},
\texttt{Rgs6}, \texttt{Nos1ap} all recur as hubs in
\texttt{snc\_intact\_network/}'s blue module too). This cross-cohort,
cross-condition consistency is independent evidence --- on top of the
formal preservation test against VTA in
\texttt{../snc\_vs\_vta\_preservation/} --- that this is a real, stable
SNc-specific co-expression program, not noise particular to one network
construction.

\subsection*{Caveats}

Module color labels are assigned by size rank at construction time and
carry no biological meaning on their own; the fact that this module is
``turquoise'' here and ``blue'' in \texttt{snc\_intact\_network/} is
incidental, not a discrepancy. kME estimates here are computed on 2{,}226
metacell-aggregated pseudo-samples pooled across 6 animals (see
\texttt{module\_by\_animal\_heatmap.png} to check no single animal is
driving the module), not 2{,}226 independent single cells, so treat the
third decimal place of any kME as more precise than the underlying biology
actually supports.
