The soft-thresholding power scan selects $\beta=5$ as the lowest power clearing
the scale-free fit threshold ($R^2 \geq 0.8$; $R^2=0.801$ at $\beta=5$, versus
$0.684$ at $\beta=4$), giving a well-behaved, non-arbitrary basis for the
network. Clustering on 2{,}226 pooled SNc cells (6 animals) yields 9 modules,
a modest number consistent with a relatively homogeneous cell population.

CACNA1D is assigned to the \textbf{turquoise} module with $\mathrm{kME}=0.442$ ---
a moderate, non-trivial connection, though still short of turquoise's
top hub genes. \emph{(Correction, 2026-08-13: this document previously
reported $\mathrm{kME}=0.048$, misread from the wrong \texttt{kME\_*} column
in \texttt{modules.csv}; 0.442 is \texttt{kME\_turquoise} for Cacna1d,
re-verified directly against the source file.)} CACNA1D is meaningfully
embedded in SNc's co-expression structure, not merely a peripheral member.

This is reinforced by the enrichment result: turquoise is significantly
enriched for the KEGG term \emph{Dopaminergic synapse}
($p_{\mathrm{adj}} = 2.77\times10^{-4}$, \texttt{KEGG\_2019\_Mouse} ---
also corrected 2026-08-13 from the wrong-species \texttt{KEGG\_2021\_Human}
library), tied with \emph{Axon guidance} at the same $p_{\mathrm{adj}}$,
directly on-topic for a dopaminergic neuron dataset. Both the module's
function and CACNA1D's moderate centrality within it point the same
direction: this is a real, biologically coherent program that CACNA1D is
substantively part of, not a coincidental grouping.
